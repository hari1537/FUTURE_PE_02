🤖 UGC Ad Content – CarePlus Clinic
📌 Project Overview

This project is part of Future Interns – Prompt Engineering Task 2 (2026).
It focuses on generating high-converting UGC (User Generated Content) style ad scripts using AI.

UGC ads feel real and relatable, helping businesses connect better with customers and improve conversions.

🏥 Business Chosen

CarePlus Clinic

A healthcare clinic providing diagnosis and treatment for common health issues.

⚠️ Problem Statement

Many people ignore symptoms like fever, body pain, or fatigue, assuming they are minor.
This delay can lead to serious health complications.

🧠 Prompt Used

Generate a UGC-style ad script for a clinic named "CarePlus Clinic". The script should feel like a real patient experience, not a sales ad. Include a strong hook, a personal story (symptoms like fever), the clinic as a solution, and a clear call to action. Keep it short, emotional, and suitable for Instagram Reels.

🎣 Hooks
I ignored my fever for 3 days…
3 days fever is not normal
Don’t ignore this symptom
This mistake made my health worse
I wish I went to the clinic earlier
🎬 UGC Ad Scripts
Script 1

Hook: I ignored my fever for 3 days…
Ad: I thought it was normal, but it got worse. Then I visited CarePlus Clinic and got proper treatment.
CTA: Don’t ignore symptoms. Visit CarePlus Clinic today.

Script 2

Hook: 3 days fever is not normal
Ad: I delayed my checkup and it became serious. CarePlus Clinic helped me recover quickly.
CTA: Book your appointment now.

Script 3

Hook: Don’t make this health mistake
Ad: Ignoring small symptoms can lead to bigger problems. CarePlus Clinic provided timely care.
CTA: Visit today for early diagnosis.

📢 Call To Action (CTA)
Visit CarePlus Clinic today
Book your appointment now
Don’t delay your health check
Get early treatment
